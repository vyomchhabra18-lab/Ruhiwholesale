Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Ruhi Wholesale explaining information used for wholesale inquiries, orders, WhatsApp communication, cookies and analytics.

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Information we receive

When you contact Ruhi Wholesale or place a wholesale inquiry, you may provide your name, phone number, delivery location, garment requirements, quantities, size or colour preferences, order details and payment-related information such as a payment screenshot.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, confirm product availability and pricing, prepare order details, coordinate delivery or transport, process communications and handle customer-service or damage claims.

3. WhatsApp and third-party services

When you choose the WhatsApp ordering option, the information you submit is transmitted to WhatsApp and handled under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices. We do not control third-party privacy practices.

4. Cookies

The website may use essential browser storage or cookies where needed for basic functionality. If non-essential cookies are introduced, they should be described and managed according to the applicable requirements.

5. Analytics

This current static site does not intentionally claim that analytics software is active. If analytics or measurement services are added later, they may collect information such as device, browser, pages viewed, approximate location and interaction data according to the provider's settings and applicable notices.

6. Data sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the website, communicate with you, process an order, arrange transport or comply with legal obligations. We do not state that personal information is sold to advertisers.

7. Data retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, business records, dispute handling or legal obligations. Reasonable administrative and technical measures should be used to protect information, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about an inquiry, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask questions about its use, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping needs.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Ruhi Wholesale at +91 9871149400 or through WhatsApp.
